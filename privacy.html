Privacy Policy

Live Wallpapers 4K (Android app hd.uhd.live.wallpapers.topwallpapers1) · Effective 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Live Wallpapers 4K app ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: the app has no user accounts, we do not run a server that collects your data, and everything you save in the app stays on your phone. Ads, purchases and the download of wallpapers involve the third-party services listed below.

What the app does

The app shows live (video and animated), glitter, clock and still 4K / HD wallpapers, sets them as your home or lock screen wallpaper, and can change your wallpaper automatically. Live wallpapers are free-licensed videos from Wikimedia Commons or animations drawn on your phone; glitter and clock wallpapers are drawn on your phone; photos come from Wikimedia Commons.

Information stored only on your phone

  • Your favourites, downloaded wallpapers, settings (such as theme, speed, glitter effects, changer interval) and simple counts of which wallpapers you viewed or set on this phone (used for the "Most Viewed / Most Downloaded" sorting).
  • Wallpaper files the app downloads so they can be shown offline.
  • If you use "Make your own" glitter wallpaper, the picture you choose with Android's photo picker is copied into the app's private storage. It is never uploaded.

We do not receive any of this information. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Information that leaves your phone

  • Wallpaper list. To show new wallpapers without an app update, the app downloads a public list of wallpapers from GitHub Pages (github.io). Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request information (for example the time and the app's name and version). The app also checks this list in the background about twice a day, so it can tell you about new wallpapers. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Photos and videos. When you open, preview, download or set a wallpaper, the app requests the picture or video from Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org and upload.wikimedia.org) or, for wallpapers added to the app's own wallpaper site, from GitHub Pages (github.io). These services receive your IP address and the file requested. See the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy and the GitHub Privacy Statement linked above.
  • Ad settings. The app downloads its ad settings (which ads may show and how often) from a small configuration service hosted on Cloudflare (workers.dev). Like any web server, it receives your IP address and basic request information. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Push messages. To send occasional news, such as new wallpapers, the app registers with Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging, which gives this installation a random token. The app also joins general groups (all users, your country and language as set on your phone, and the app version) so messages can be relevant. No account or contact details are involved. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.
  • Advertising. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (banners, full-screen ads and, if you choose, reward videos that remove ads for a while). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), your IP address, approximate location derived from it, and how you interact with ads. Ads are not personalised unless you turn on "Personalised ads" yourself; it is off by default. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), Google's consent form asks for your choice first. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Purchases. Pro is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; the app only learns whether your Google account owns Pro. We never see your card or payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your information, do not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and do not share information with anyone else.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state – to download wallpapers, the wallpaper list, ads and purchase status.
  • Set wallpaper – to apply the wallpaper you choose.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and newer: asked once) – to tell you when new wallpapers are added and to show occasional news (push messages, see above). Turn them off, or their sound and vibration, in Settings › Notifications or in Android's settings for the app.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) – to save a wallpaper to your Pictures folder when you tap Download. Newer Android versions need no permission for this.
  • Motion sensor – read on the phone to move wallpapers slightly when you tilt it (parallax). The readings never leave the phone.

The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or files other than the picture you pick yourself.

Your choices

  • Turn personalised ads on or off at any time in Settings › Personalised ads. Where available, Settings › Privacy choices reopens Google's consent form.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Buying Pro removes ads entirely.
  • To delete everything the app stored, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. There is no account to delete.

Security

All downloads use encrypted HTTPS connections. Information stored by the app stays in its private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and in the app, with a new effective date.

Contact
